A97 CRMA CRM for the sales and marketing teams, giving admins full visibility into every action across the company.DetailsClose

In real estate a good lead rarely belongs to one agent, but most CRMs keep a single status per lead, so each update erases the last. In this CRM each sales agent keeps a private pipeline on the lead, and the admin sees all of them side by side. Live and in daily use.

  • Many agents, one lead. An 11-step feedback pipeline from New to Contract signed. Signing closes the lead for everyone.
  • Each role sees only what it needs. Sales can't see who brought the lead in or who else is working it, so client details stay confidential and agents stay competitive.
  • Every promise on time. A diary, call-back reminders, and one tap to call or WhatsApp a client from the phone.
  • Full traceability. An audit trail nobody can edit on every lead, sales performance and win rates, and team leaders who run their own teams.
  • Hardened. A security audit found and fixed 30 issues. Six checks run on every request, sessions end when a password changes, and duplicate phone numbers are caught.

3,400+ real leads managed · 6 roles, enforced on the server · 11 pipeline steps.

Node.js, Express, SQLite, plain JavaScript, Docker, Render, off-site backup every 15 minutes.

A97 Lead GeneratorA web platform that sells fresh real estate leads to brokers for points.DetailsClose

Staff add leads, and buyers browse them for free, seeing the area, project, unit, budget and payment plan. They spend points to unlock the client's name, full number and notes. Each lead is sold once: the buyer who unlocks it owns it, and it disappears from everyone else's list.

  • Nothing for sale leaves the server early. The browse list is built from columns that exclude the name, phone and notes, and tests check every response.
  • A balance is its history. Points live only in a ledger of top-ups, unlocks, refunds and corrections, and every change runs in one transaction.
  • Race-proof. Two buyers who press Unlock at the same moment can't both get the lead, and the second one isn't charged.
  • Fair refunds. Buyers report a wrong number within 48 hours, and staff refund it or reject it with a reason.

5 roles · 74 automated tests · 0 inline scripts, strict CSP.

Node.js, Express, SQLite, JWT and bcrypt, CSV import and Excel export, Docker.

A97 Finance TrackerA private finance app for the company's income, expenses and debts.DetailsClose

The first product I built at A97. It gave management one trusted place to record every transaction and see where the money goes, in place of scattered notes and spreadsheets.

  • Income and expenses by category, with search, single-day or date-range filters, and a chart of the top spending categories.
  • Debts both ways, what the company owes and what it is owed, with due dates, partial payments, what is still to settle, and overdue flags.
  • Nothing is lost. Deleted records can be shown and restored, and every entry records who added it and who last edited it.
  • Invite-only access with admin and editor roles, invite links to set a password, and signed sessions.
  • Export to CSV and Excel, plus backup and restore scripts. The data lives in MongoDB Atlas, so it survives restarts and redeploys.

Node.js, Express, MongoDB Atlas, Chart.js, JWT and bcrypt.

A97 InfinityLed a team of 3 developers to build a property marketplace with map search, unit comparison and client listings.DetailsClose

The public a97homes.com platform, in Arabic and English, where clients search, compare and list properties, with a full admin dashboard behind it. One API with 222 endpoints in five parts: public search, owners, signed-in users and a back office with role-based permissions.

  • Map search for units and compounds, filtered by city, developer, type, price, area, delivery date and payment plan, with autocomplete.
  • Side-by-side comparison of two to four units or compounds, similar-compound suggestions, and construction phases.
  • Client listings. Owners submit units for sale, and staff approve or reject them.
  • Tools for buyers. A mortgage calculator, an AI chatbot, favourites, saved searches with email alerts, and reviews.
  • Secure by design. Password reset by one-time code that never reveals which emails exist, and sign-out on every device.

222 API endpoints · 5 API modules · AR · EN two languages.

Laravel, Sanctum, REST API, role-based access, Postman.
