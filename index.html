A97 CRM

A real estate CRM where several sales agents can work the same lead, and the admin watches every one of them.

In real estate a good lead rarely belongs to one agent, but most CRMs keep a single status per lead, so each update erases the last. In this CRM each sales agent keeps a private pipeline on the lead, and the admin sees all of them side by side.

  • Many agents, one lead. An 11-step feedback pipeline from New to Contract signed. Signing closes the lead for everyone.
  • Each role sees only what it needs. Sales can't see who brought the lead in or who else is working it, so client details stay confidential and agents stay competitive.
  • Every promise on time. A diary, call-back reminders, and one tap to call or WhatsApp a client from the phone.
  • Full traceability. A tamper-proof audit trail on every lead, sales performance and win rates, and team leaders who run their own teams.
  • Hardened. A security audit found and fixed 30 issues. Six checks run on every request, sessions are revoked on password change, and duplicate phone numbers are caught.

A97 Lead Generator

A web platform that sells fresh real estate leads to brokers for points.

Staff add leads, and buyers browse them for free, seeing the area, project, unit, budget and payment plan. They spend points to unlock the client's name, full number and notes. Each lead is sold once: the buyer who unlocks it owns it, and it disappears from everyone else's list.

  • Nothing for sale leaves the server early. The browse list is built from columns that exclude the name, phone and notes, and tests check every payload.
  • A balance is its history. Points live only in a ledger of top-ups, unlocks, refunds and corrections, and every move runs in one transaction.
  • Race-proof. A unique index means two buyers who press Unlock at once can't both get the lead, and the second one isn't charged.
  • Fair refunds. Buyers report a wrong number within 48 hours, and staff refund or reject it with a reason.
  • Attack-tested. Forged tokens, role escalation, SQL injection, stored XSS, double refunds, and 300 random operations after which the books must balance.

A97 Finance Tracker

A private finance app for the company's income, expenses and debts.

It's the first product I built at A97. It gave management one trusted place to record every transaction and see where the money goes, in place of scattered notes and spreadsheets.

  • Income and expenses by category, with search, single-day or date-range filters, and a "Top spending categories" chart.
  • Debt tracking both ways, "I owe" and "Owed to me", with due dates, partial payments, what's still to settle, and overdue flags.
  • Nothing is lost. Deleted records can be shown and restored, and every entry records who added and last edited it.
  • Invite-only access with admin and editor roles, invite links to set a password, and JWT sessions.
  • Export to CSV and Excel, plus backup and restore scripts. The data lives in MongoDB Atlas, so it survives restarts and redeploys.

A97 Infinity: property marketplace

The public a97homes.com platform where clients search, compare and list properties, with a full admin dashboard behind it.

I led a team of three developers to build A97's client-facing marketplace in Arabic and English. Behind it is one API with 222 endpoints in five modules, covering public search, owners, signed-in users and a back office with role-based permissions.

  • Interactive map search for units and compounds, with filters for city, developer, type, price, area, delivery date and payment plan, plus autocomplete.
  • Side-by-side comparison of two to four units or compounds, similar-compound suggestions, and compound phases.
  • Client property listings. Owners submit units for sale, and staff approve or reject the orders.
  • Tools for buyers. A mortgage calculator, an AI chatbot assistant, favourites, saved searches with email alerts, notifications, and reviews of compounds and consultants.
  • Admin dashboard for developers, compounds, units, payment plans, offers, FAQs, articles, the newsletter, static pages, and roles and permissions.
  • Secure by design. Sanctum tokens, password reset by OTP that doesn't reveal which emails exist, sign-out on every device, and IDs that can't be enumerated.
