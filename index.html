A97 · LIVE · 30 SECURITY FIXES

A97 CRM

In real estate a good lead rarely belongs to one agent, but most CRMs keep a single status per lead, so each update erases the last. In this CRM each sales agent keeps a private pipeline on the lead, and the admin sees all of them side by side.

  • Many agents, one lead. An 11-step feedback pipeline from New to Contract signed. Signing closes the lead for everyone.
  • Each role sees only what it needs. Sales can't see who brought the lead in or who else is working it.
  • Every promise on time. A diary, call-back reminders, and one tap to call or WhatsApp a client from the phone.
  • Full traceability. An audit trail nobody can edit, sales performance and win rates, and team leaders who run their teams.
  • Hardened. A security audit found and fixed 30 issues, including an account-takeover chain.

A97 · 74 TESTS

A97 Lead Generator

Staff add leads, and buyers browse them for free: area, project, unit, budget and payment plan. They spend points to unlock the client's name, full number and notes. Each lead is sold once: the buyer who unlocks it owns it, and it leaves everyone else's list.

  • Nothing for sale leaves the server early. The browse list never contains the name, phone or notes, and tests check every response.
  • A balance is its history. Points live only in a ledger of top-ups, unlocks, refunds and corrections.
  • Race-proof. Two buyers pressing Unlock at once can't both get the lead, and the second isn't charged.
  • Fair refunds. Buyers report a wrong number within 48 hours, and staff refund it or reject it with a reason.
  • Attack-tested: forged tokens, role escalation, SQL injection, stored XSS and double refunds.

CyberVault

A local-first web app for keeping penetration-testing knowledge and running practice engagements. Notes are organised as Domain → Category → Topic with Markdown, code, Mermaid and Excalidraw blocks, and the whole library is shown as a mind map.
